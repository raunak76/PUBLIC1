Approach: pairwise correlation templates + annealed assignment decoding

Idea
Electrode identity is recovered from how each channel co-varies with every other
channel. Nearby scalp electrodes share signal, and this relationship transfers
across people much better than any single channel's own spectrum (a per-channel
spectral classifier was near chance in development). The task is therefore
treated as a quadratic assignment problem: find the permutation of the 64 labels
that maximises the likelihood of all 64x64 channel-pair relationships, with the
FP1 and O2 anchor rows fixed.

Pair features (deterministic, per packet only)
For every channel pair, 23 numbers are computed from the half-second window:
Fisher-z correlations of the raw signal, first and second differences, the
common-average re-referenced signal (and its difference), the signal with 1, 2
or 3 leading principal components removed, band-limited signals (0-4, 4-8, 8-13,
13-30, 30-80, 30-50, 50-80 Hz, and 0-4 / 8-13 / 13-30 Hz after common-average
referencing), alpha and beta envelope correlations, the symmetric lag-1
correlation and antisymmetric lag-1 and lag-2 correlations (phase lead/lag).

Model
For each ordered electrode pair (a, b) a multivariate Gaussian over the 23
features is fitted on the labeled training + validation packets. Each pair's
covariance is shrunk strongly toward the covariance pooled over all pairs
(shrinkage weight 1000 pseudo-samples, plus a small ridge). The log-likelihood
is written as a linear function of quadratic pair features, so the full score
tensor W[i, a, j, b] for a packet is one matrix product.

Decoding
Simulated annealing over swaps of the 62 unanchored rows maximises the sum of
pair log-likelihoods. Swap gains are evaluated in O(1) from a maintained row x
label context matrix. The starting temperature is set from the spread of random
swap gains in that packet; 10 restarts x 500k proposals, best score kept. Each
packet is decoded independently with a fixed seed; no test statistics, no
cross-packet linking, no test-time fitting. The output is a permutation by
construction, anchors are preserved, and both are asserted before writing.

Development
Metric reproduced locally (accuracy over the 62 unanchored rows, mean over
packets). Feature sets, shrinkage and annealing settings were chosen with
6-fold participant-grouped cross-validation over the 72 labeled participants
(train + validation pooled, folds split by subject_group). Tried and rejected:
per-channel spectral unary model, left/right mirror augmentation, and
discriminative swap-margin fine-tuning (all lowered held-out accuracy).

Runtime
CPU only, numpy/scipy only, up to 10 worker processes. About 9 minutes on
4 cores end to end; well inside the 5,400 s limit.
